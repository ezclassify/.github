# Code of Conduct

EzClassify is built by people who like making good software. What holds this
community together is the work itself: contributing to open source and
shipping solutions that make people's lives easier. Everything in this
document follows from that.

## The standard

Treat everyone here with professional respect. Harassment and discrimination
have no place in this community, full stop. What makes us different from one
another is never a basis for how anyone gets treated here; contributions are
judged on their merits.

Behavior that has no place here:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks of any kind
- Insults, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussion
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces
- Any conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions,
review comments, and anywhere someone represents the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or contributors that violate
this standard. Report conduct issues privately to support@ezclassify.com;
reports are reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. Maintainers are
held to the same standard.
